    One workshop, from the first dataset to the tenth release.

    1. i

      Dataset

      We collect what your company knows, clean and label it, and account for every source.

      You receive a dataset card and a source manifest.

    2. ii

      Pre-training

      When general models do not know your field, we continue pre-training on your own corpus.

      You receive training runs and logs.

    3. iii

      Post-training

      Fine-tuning and preference tuning on the tasks your people actually do, tested against an evaluation you agree to.

      You receive weights and an evaluation report.

    4. iv

      Deployment

      Delivered where it must run: your data center, your cloud or your devices.

      You receive a versioned model and a runbook.

    5. v

      Upkeep

      Monitoring, retraining on new material and new releases for as long as you use it.

      You receive a change log for every release.

    Commission a modelbiz@random-walk.co.jp

    i. Dataset

    Beneath the paint, the drawing.

    Infrared shows the drawing under a painting. Under a model it is the data: your contracts, tickets and manuals, collected, cleaned, labelled and accounted for.

    You receive a dataset card and a source manifest.

    ii. Pre-training

    Beneath the drawing, an earlier picture.

    X-rays find the picture a painting was made over. Most models start from one too: we choose an open base model and continue pre-training it on your field until it knows the vocabulary.

    You receive training runs, logs and the base model’s licence.

    iii. Post-training and evaluation

    Light from the side shows every stroke.

    We fine-tune on the tasks your people actually do, then test every release against an evaluation set you agree to, including what the model must refuse.

    You receive weights and an evaluation report.

    iv. Deployment and upkeep

    Ultraviolet shows every later retouch.

    We deliver the model where it must run: your data center, your cloud or your devices. Then we monitor it, retrain it on new material and record every release.

    You receive a versioned model, a runbook and a change log.
